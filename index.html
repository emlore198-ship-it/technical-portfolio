01 / Kinematics

FABRIK

A procedural kinematic locomotion solver

Inverse kinematicsIterative constraintsGait scheduling

A procedural locomotion system that coordinates articulated legs through persistent foot state, phase-based gait scheduling, and terrain queries. I implemented the FABRIK algorithm as sequential backward and forward sweeps to enforce segment-length constraints, then converted the solved chains into oriented geometry. The study documents the system architecture, adjustable topology, and the limits of its kinematic model.

02 / Agent-based systems

Physarum

Local rules. Shared memory. Emergent patterns.

Agent-based simulationLocal sensingField feedback

A Physarum-inspired simulation in which agents sample a shared scent field, update their headings, and leave deposits that influence later evaluations. I built a persistent feedback loop between individual agent state and environmental memory, with controls for population, sensing, movement, and field updates. The study examines the implemented rules and their observed behavior; its output is an evolving field rather than an extracted shortest path.

03 / Computational mathematics

Fractals

From recurrence to geometry

Geometric recursionProbabilistic affine mapsComplex iteration

Ten mathematical generators brought together in one procedural system, spanning recursive geometry, probabilistic affine maps, and complex iteration. I translated established constructions—including Sierpinski forms, the Barnsley fern, and Mandelbrot and Julia sets—into inspectable node graphs. The study connects their mathematical rules to generated geometry and examines how depth, sampling resolution, and iteration count affect computational growth.
